Frame Geometry Procedure
========================

UUID: ``53FEB0B0-95AB-405B-827D-B255FE50D192``

1. Identify the approved frame-geometry contract and capture conditions.
2. Capture complete frames with the associated identity and status information.
3. Confirm that each accepted frame preserves all 3648 effective pixels in
   acquisition order.
4. Compare effective-pixel boundaries and any non-effective regions with the
   approved contract.
5. Record mismatches as unresolved requirements or failed checks; do not infer
   missing samples or promote an observed layout into a requirement.
6. Link the result to the requirement, hardware configuration, and captured
   evidence revisions.
